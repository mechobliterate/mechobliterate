## hey, i'm josiah. 👋

**Creative technologist, software engineer, entrepreneur, and writer based in New York City.**

I've been building things on the web since before I really knew what I was doing. Some ideas became products, some became companies, and others became lessons worth writing about.

I'm interested in what happens when engineering, design, and human behavior meet. I like figuring out why things work, why they don't, and how to make them better.

*Building things, questioning ideas, and figuring out everything in between.*

### what i'm working on

- **Play Games Interactive:** Building an independent gaming and technology company focused on multiplayer experiences, custom infrastructure, and the systems behind them.
- **Writing:** Thinking out loud about technology, entrepreneurship, building products, and growing up alongside the things I create.
- **Small experiments:** Exploring developer tools, interfaces, languages, and whatever else catches my attention.

### a few things i've built

- **Analyse / Tebex Analytics:** Founded an analytics platform for game communities that went on to process over 2 billion records. Analyse was acquired by Tebex, where I continued developing the product as Head of Analytics.
- **Geode Host:** Co-founded a hosting company and led product work on a real-time server management panel and infrastructure serving U.S. and European regions.
- **Silica:** A Swift-inspired programming language interpreter written in C++.
- **Giftbox:** Interactive digital gifts, built with TypeScript.

I've also spent time working at the intersection of AI, product, and content at Pinterest, and cybersecurity and risk at Deloitte.

### things i care about

Thoughtful software. Fast, dependable infrastructure. Developer experience. Good interfaces. Attention to detail. Asking better questions before rushing into answers.

And writing, because building something and understanding what it means are two different kinds of work.
